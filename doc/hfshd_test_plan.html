Can the sheath drive HFSHD?

The boundary condition works — closure to 0.00 % over 3900 steps. The mechanism does not, at this drive: a 36 V imposed target-to-target bias rearranges plasma along the inner leg but moves its line-integrated content by 1.5 %. The drift reshapes profiles; it does not transfer plasma across the private flux region. What is left to test is whether a recycling divertor amplifies that, because everything so far has been measured in one that cannot.

WHY

Two regimes, never both at once

HFSHD needs a potential drive and a recycling divertor to amplify it. Every run so far has had one or the other.

The direct drift contribution is small: ΓE×B ≈ 8.7×1021 against Γ∥ ≈ 7.3×1022 m⁻²s⁻¹, about 12 %. In experiment HFSHD is a factor 2–10, reached because recycling feedback amplifies a modest drift. At 1018 m⁻³ that gain is ≈ 1, so 12 % stays 12 %.

00

Fix before running anything

Two config defects that silently weaken exactly the effect being looked for. Neither needs a rebuild.

blocks everything

Polygon vertex order

All four polygons — both valves, both pumps — are listed in bowtie order, so inside_polygon ray-casting returns two triangles instead of a rectangle. Porting the routine and sampling gives 50.5 / 30.1 / 50.3 / 50.5 % of the intended boxes. Both pumps are at half strength and the wrong shape.

valves(1)%poly_Z = -1.11, -1.05, -1.05, -1.11   ! was -1.11, -1.05, -1.11, -1.05
valves(2)%poly_Z = -1.11, -1.05, -1.09, -1.15
P1     %poly_Z = -1.14, -1.12, -1.12, -1.14
P2     %poly_Z = -1.2,  -1.151, -1.151, -1.2

mod_particle_wall_interaction.f90:83 warns about this explicitly. valves(2) only reaches 60 % even when ordered — it is a slanted quadrilateral, which may be intended.

blocks everything

T_min_sheath is unset

It defaults to −1, so the sheath falls back to T_min_neg = 3e-5, whose knee sits at 1.474 eV — above the whole cold-divertor range.

Only meaningful once 00 and 02 are done, and it is the run the whole campaign has been building toward.

decisive

Bias scan inside the high-recycling regime

Repeat the 01 scan at the operating point found in 02. The slope measured so far — 0.005–0.009 per volt — comes from a sheath-limited divertor where the recycling gain is ≈ 1 and nothing amplifies the drift. That is why the 160 V extrapolation looks discouraging, and why it is probably pessimistic.

how much larger the slope is. It needs to rise by ~70× for the self-consistent −23 V to give a factor-2 asymmetry. That is a lot, but a genuinely high-recycling divertor can have an ionisation gain in that range — and the present measurement was taken where the gain is ≈ 1.

03

Restore the geometry

realism

Asymmetric pumping

Both pumps currently sit at weight_factor = 0.98 — deliberately symmetric. In AUG the cryopump is in the outer lower corner and the inner divertor is more closed, so neutrals that recycle inboard stay inboard. A large part of real HFSHD is that asymmetry, and it is absent by construction.

P1 (inner) %weight_factor = 0.995d0   ! nearly closed
P2 (outer) %weight_factor = 0.90d0    ! the cryopump

Bias the sink, not the source. Puffing into the inner divertor to produce HFSHD is circular if the goal is to show that drifts cause it — it imposes the answer through the source term and then measures it.

04

Missing physics

Where to look if test 01 comes back flat. The first is already written and simply on another branch.

  • Thermoelectric Ohm term — the 0.71∇∥Te thermal force, which drives current between targets that differ in Te. Exists on u-bcs-tests-mate, absent here. Cherry-pick.
  • ∇·(0.71 Te j∥/e) — the thermoelectric heat flux in the electron energy equation. In SOLPS, not in model600. Transfers energy between targets whenever current flows.
  • ∇B terms in the energy equations — also present in SOLPS, absent in model600, and acting in the in–out channel.
  • diamag_heat_conv — the ±Γ·2τ′·∂Z(nTs²) term, the Poletaeva XPR mechanism. Written but uncommitted in the working tree.
05

Watch while running

  • Shock-capturing smearing Er — use_sc with D_perp_sc_num = 10 adds diffusion where gradients are steep, which is the target region. One run at a reduced value shows whether the potential structure sharpens. Do not remove it blind; it is what made the BC stable.
  • Φ along a field line — the SOL value follows from parallel electron force balance, e∇∥Φ ≈ ∇∥pe/n + 0.71∇∥Te. Trace it target-to-upstream with pol_line. Flat where it should not be means the transmission is wrong, not the boundary condition.
  • PFR drift direction — util/analyse_pfr_line.py. Outer → inner is the mechanism working; inner → outer means it actively opposes.
06

Verify the BC itself

Shelved, but worth knowing what the current diagnostics cannot catch: I_sheath and I_Ampère share the same zj·(B·n)/(F₀μ₀) conversion, so a normalisation error cancels in their ratio. A factor of two in a_n passes every check now printed.

  • Circular limiter, uniform Te — no thermoelectric drive means no current, so the characteristic requires X = 0, i.e. ePhi/kTe = Λ exactly. Reads straight off the existing log.
  • Λ-scan — Φ must be linear in Λ with slope exactly Te/e. The absolute check a units error cannot survive.
  • j–V collapse — plot j/jsat against X for every boundary point; all must land on f(X) with no free parameters. Works on existing exports.
  • h-convergence — refine the boundary and watch the closure error. A plateau means the trace assembly is inconsistent.
XX

Falsified — do not retry

Each of these cost at least one run.

  • sheath_weak_relax < 1 — worse on every metric at timeout; the alternation it targeted was a transient that cleared itself.
  • sheath_X_min below −3 — actively harmful. X_min is the clamp on the electron branch, so −6 raises the floor on f from −19 to −445.
  • Boundary-only E×B direction — direction coherence 0.26 and 0.001 on the inner leg. The transfer happens in the PFR volume; use a traverse.
  • Sheath on boundary types 1 + 4 — four attempts, including a seam guard that was a real bug fix and changed nothing. Two sheath surfaces close a circuit through u with no damping in the loop.
  • ψN as a PFR/SOL coordinate — it exceeds 1 on both sides of a separatrix leg. Use signed arc length from the strike point.
  • sheath_wall_vel, sheath_init_u_all, β penalty, the t_min resistivity clamp — all falsified earlier in the sheath BC work.
